## 1. Project summary

Magyar Közgazdászok (`gbekes/magyar-kozgazdaszok` on GitHub) is a
static, bilingual catalogue of peer-reviewed research by top
Hungarian economists, framed for policymakers. JSON content layer in
`data/`, plain HTML/JS pages, deploys via GitHub Pages on every push
to `main`. As of this handover the catalogue carries **99 authors and
811 papers**; HU summary coverage is **445/811 (54.9 %)** — i.e.
342 papers have an English summary but no Hungarian translation yet.

  possible if she shared the link widely. So far, low priority.

- **License consequences**. The catalogue licence switched today
  from "MIT (code) + CC BY 4.0 (content)" to a unified
  CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The CC-BY-NC-SA 4.0 is more restrictive
  (`NonCommercial`, `ShareAlike`); any prior re-users of the old
  permissive content keep their CC-BY 4.0 grant on the snapshot they
  took, but new content is under the new licence. Worth flagging in
  the contribute.html if anyone asks.
